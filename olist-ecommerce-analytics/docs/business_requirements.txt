================================================================================
BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS & TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
Project: Olist E-Commerce Analytics & Database Engineering
Database Engine: MySQL 8.0+
Dataset: Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset
================================================================================

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The primary objective of this project is to build an end-to-end analytical 
database solution for Olist (a Brazilian e-commerce marketplace platform). 
The project addresses executive reporting needs, transactional metric alignment,
support automation, order lifecycle auditing, and database system performance 
maintenance.


2. COMPLETED SPRINT REQUIREMENTS & DELIVERABLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[REQ-01] Exploratory Data Analysis & Metric Alignment (Sprints 1–3)
- Business Need: Reconcile transactional revenue differences between order items 
  and payment records, and perform window-function analytics across orders.
- Deliverable: SQL analysis scripts in `02_exploratory_data_analysis.sql` establishing 
  item-level gross sales (`price + freight_value`) parity with basket payment values.

[REQ-02] Executive KPI & Regional Sales Tracking (Sprint 4 - Task 1)
- Business Need: Executive leadership requires high-level visibility into regional 
  sales performance and Average Order Value (AOV) across Brazilian states.
- Deliverable: Optimized database view (`vw_regional_sales_summary`) aggregating 
  monthly gross sales for delivered orders without item-level fan-out inflation.

[REQ-03] Customer Support Order History Lookup (Sprint 4 - Task 2)
- Business Need: Support and operations teams need a fast, standardized mechanism 
  to audit full purchasing history for specific customers by unique ID.
- Deliverable: Parameterized stored procedure (`sp_get_customer_history`) featuring 
  defensive validation (`EXISTS` check) and explicit error signaling (`SIGNAL SQLSTATE`).

[REQ-04] Order Lifecycle Audit Trail (Sprint 5 - Task 1)
- Business Need: Logistics teams require historical tracking of order status 
  transitions (e.g., shipped -> delivered) for SLA enforcement and dispute handling.
- Deliverable: Append-only audit table (`order_status_audit`) and an automated 
  row-level trigger (`trg_log_order_status_change`) on the `orders` table.

[REQ-05] Automated System Maintenance & Log Retention (Sprint 5 - Task 2)
- Business Need: Continuous logging generates storage overhead. Audit records older 
  than 30 days must be purged automatically without manual DBA intervention.
- Deliverable: Recurring background event (`evt_daily_sales_cleanup`) configured 
  to run daily at midnight to enforce a 30-day log retention window.


3. DATA MODELING & TECHNICAL STANDARDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Primary Key Integrity: Enforce unique surrogate/natural keys across orders, 
  customers, sellers, and items datasets.
- Order Gross Sales Standard: Gross revenue must consistently evaluate as 
  `SUM(price + freight_value)` at the item level to maintain transactional parity.
- Parameter Shadowing Prevention: All procedural inputs use explicit prefixes 
  (e.g., `p_customer_unique_id`) to avoid column-name collisions in SQL queries.
- Modular Execution: All database objects include defensive drop/creation statements 
  (`DROP VIEW/PROCEDURE/TRIGGER/EVENT IF EXISTS`) for idempotent deployment.


4. SUCCESS METRICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Zero item duplication/fan-out in executive sales aggregation queries.
- Instant error response (`SQLSTATE '45000'`) on non-existent customer lookups.
- Automated 100% capture rate of order status mutations in the audit ledger.
- Zero manual DBA effort required for log retention management.
